About me

My name is Renaline P. Malazarte, and I am a UI/UX designer in training who enjoys creating clean, visually appealing, and user-friendly designs. I believe that a good design should not only look great but also be easy to understand and comfortable to use on both phones and computers.

I enjoy exploring how people interact with apps and websites, then organizing layouts, choosing colors, and arranging text to create a smooth and enjoyable user experience. I am continuously learning new design tools, improving my skills, and exploring creative ways to make digital experiences more intuitive and accessible.

As I continue to grow as a designer, I look forward to gaining new experiences, learning from others, and creating meaningful designs that meet users' needs.
